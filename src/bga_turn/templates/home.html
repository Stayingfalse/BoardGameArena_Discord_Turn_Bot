How it works

Three steps, no slash commands required.

1

Add the bot

Invite it to your server. It needs to read messages and post in the channels where you share games.

2

Share a table link

Paste any BGA table link. The bot posts a recruiting card and keeps it updated as seats fill.

3

Link players

Players click Link your BGA & Discord once, and get @mentioned whenever it's their turn.
